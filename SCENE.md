## North star

In the repo's own words: *your AI agents already write a journal — prose reads it,
so you catch up in seconds, not scrollback.*

The journals already exist. Nothing here is about capturing more; it is about the
catching-up being **instant, verbatim, and honest** — cheap enough to run before
you think, and never a summary you have to go behind.

## Ideal users

**An agent opening a session, orienting before it acts.** It has no memory of
yesterday and is one tool call away from guessing instead of knowing. It runs a
verb, reads what comes back, and starts work. It will not cross-check the answer —
whatever prose says is what it believes. This is the load-bearing user: the whole
tool is upstream of somebody else's first move.

**Lars, asking where we were.** Speech-to-text, casual, mid-thought. He wants the
gist of yesterday without opening a transcript, and he will not dig for it. If the
answer takes longer than his patience, he switches off — and a tool that is off is
worth nothing regardless of how right it would have been.

## The feeling

Sensory, not archival. A verb should feel like glancing up, not like querying a
database — instant, cheap, no key, no setup, no thinking about it beforehand. The
pure verbs stay pure: no LLM in the path you reach for reflexively.

## What it says no to

- **No invented recall.** Verbatim or nothing. A plausible paraphrase of what was
  said is worse than silence, because the reader cannot tell the difference.
- **No orienting on yourself.** A session must never be served its own live
  conversation as context about the past — the mirror failure, closed 2026-08-09
  in `9bd6dd8`.
- **No LLM in the reflexive path.** The daily drivers stay pure reads: free,
  offline, instant.
